# Stage 06 - The Eternal Storm Approaches

Status: campaign foundation ready, playable map pending player story brief.

Unlocked creature levels: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.
Locked creature levels: 7, 8.

Focus: Storm Dragons and Eternal Storm Dragons. The player learns speed control and the price of feeding a creature that lives on lightning and rare resources.

Map target: chained islands or high passes connected by storm portals. The scenario should take about 30 minutes and end after capturing three storm portals and claiming the storm relic.
