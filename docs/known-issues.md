# Known issues

## Town artwork

The city is still being revised. Several utility buildings are small at the native 800-pixel town width. In the current geometry, special6 is 28×30 pixels, special5 is 32×30 and special2 is 34×34 before export padding. Correct mask placement does not by itself make these structures readable or match the art direction of the original towns.

The working art constraints remain: preserve the approved background and brightness; use one ground anchor per upgrade family; keep ground-contact footprints within their terrace; use consistent perspective, material and edge treatment; retain the 30% fortification size progression. A finished visual review must assess actual town windows, not a composition preview.

No building redraw is claimed by the 1.5.10 editorial patch. Earlier game screenshots remain representative of the artwork.

## Campaign acceptance

Individual victory objectives have been reached in all eight chapters across separate automated trials. The final tests of chapters 5 and 7 used a declared priority for normal paid construction of a required mission building, followed by AI play. They ended normally with zero ERROR, while AI and test-configuration warnings remained. This is not a continuous manual campaign playthrough.

Hero and army carryover between chapters has not been certified. The earlier headless human-slot test crashed after victories in 4 and 6; the private test client was corrected for later trials. This is not presented as a verified crash in normal graphical human play.

## Language and mechanics

Eight language dictionaries are present. New biographies were drafted and edited with AI assistance; native-speaker literary review is pending. English is the source language. The custom engine requirement remains; this patch does not establish stock VCMI compatibility.
